# djSports web — User Guide

## 1. Get started
1. Open djSports and click **Login with Spotify** (Premium needed to play).
2. First time in this browser: on the welcome screen, enter the **Profile
   name + 4-digit PIN** you use in the djSports app and click **Check for
   backups → Open Cloud Backup → Full restore**. All playlists, tracks and
   start times arrive.
   No backup yet? Load the **Example Setup**, or create a playlist with **+**.
3. The chip in the top bar shows `you → djSports` in green when this tab is
   ready to play.

## 2. Home
- Type chips at the top: **All** shows one row per type, a type shows a grid.
- Drag a card to reorder it within its type — that is the order in Let's Play.
- Click a card (or its pencil) to edit; ⋮ → Open in Spotify / Delete.

## 3. Playlists and tracks
- **Show details** in the playlist editor: paste a Spotify playlist link and
  click **Sync** to import the tracks (again later to pick up new ones).
  **Search** adds single tracks; **Browse** lists the linked Spotify playlist.
- **Shuffle at end**, **Auto next** and **Position** control Let's Play.
- Click a track to set its **start time**: slider (±0.5 s), ▶ to preview,
  **Auto Preview** plays from the new point when you let go, **Set as start**
  takes the playing position. **Update & next** walks through the playlist.

## 4. Let's Play
- Click a tile to play its current track from the start time; the tile
  flashes. ‹ › (or swipe) browses without playing.
- Controls: play, pause, **fade pause** (set a fade time in Settings),
  volume ±5 %, help, **EXIT**.
- Keyboard (turn on in Settings): Hotspot **1–6**, Match **Q–Y**, Fun **A–H**,
  **P** play, **Esc** pause, **+/−** volume.

## 5. The djSports player
This browser tab is a Spotify Connect device called **djSports**. Keep the
tab open while you play. The panel at the bottom shows the track; drag its
top edge to resize, ⌄ to collapse. To play on another device instead, choose
**Settings → Spotify output → Spotify device**.

## 6. Settings
Spotify output, preferred device, Let's Play settings, dark/light theme,
the **start-time list** (copy as JSON, paste to import, fill tracks without
a start time, delete), **playlist sharing** (copy/import as JSON) and Cloud
Backup.

## 7. Cloud Backup
Same **Profile + PIN** on every device = the same backups (app and web).
**Backup Now** keeps the last 5 per device. **Full restore** replaces all
local data; **Sync (↓)** only adds playlists you don't have yet.
